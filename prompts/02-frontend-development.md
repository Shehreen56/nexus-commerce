# Frontend Development Prompts

**Authenticity:** Reconstructed from development conversation.

## Prompt 1 — Storefront and Product Catalog

**Prompt:**
Develop a responsive React and Vite e-commerce storefront with product cards, product images, prices, search, category filtering, and product details.

**Purpose:** Implement the customer-facing shopping experience.

**Outcome:** Storefront and catalog features implemented.

**Verification:** User-reported manual tests for homepage, images, search, category filters, and product details passed.

## Prompt 2 — Shopping Cart

**Prompt:**
Implement cart functionality with add/remove products, quantity controls, and automatic subtotal and total calculations.

**Purpose:** Support customer shopping operations.

**Outcome:** Cart functionality implemented.

**Verification:** User-reported add-to-cart, quantity, subtotal, and total tests passed.
